## Authentication in tests

- Send header **`api_key`** on every request (value issued for your tenant in that environment).
- Scopes in the Partner API are grouped in the OpenAPI tags **`Partner`**, **`Partner Send`**, and **`Partner Full`**. Use an API key that includes the scope required by the endpoint you are calling.

<Tip>
Keep `api_key` on your **server** only. Do not embed it in mobile apps or public web clients.
</Tip>

## Tokenized test cards (sandbox)

The platform stores **tokens** (not raw PANs). The values below are **illustrative** for sandbox-style integrations—always obtain current test tokens from your **card capture widget** or from **Leap** for your tenant and processor, since sandbox assets can rotate.

| Purpose | Example `number` (token) | Example `securityCode` (token) | Notes |
|---------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|--------|
| Generic Visa sandbox card | `tok_sandbox_9PNgfqqpdpyQpnD1jYkdw1_1111` | e.g. `tok_sandbox_4G3wTXXpqCQVct3n2oZHH9` (varies by flow) | Typical shape returned after tokenizing a test Visa in sandbox. |

Typical card fields in requests (names may vary by endpoint):

- **`cardtype`**: `CreditCard` or `DebitCard`
- **`cardNetwork`**: `Visa`, etc.
- **`expirationDate`**: `MMYY` format as required by your integration guide
- **`nameOnCard`**, **`country`**: must align with corridor / tenant rules

<Note>
If tokenization fails or the processor rotates test assets, you will get **4xx/5xx** with a `reason` (and often `code`) in the JSON body. Retry with a freshly tokenized card from your payment widget or test harness.
</Note>

## UBN and bank-style test data

For `POST /user/funding/source/ubn-account/add/{userToken}` (and related flows), sandbox account numbers are often **numeric strings** in a fixed length for your country (for example a value shaped like `002320275621938815`). **Validate** the exact test account list for your tenant and country with Leap—do not assume one global test account across all partners.

## Phones, users, and contacts

- Use **E.164** phone numbers with the correct `countryCode` for `getUserTokenV2` and registration flows.
- Prefer **synthetic** identities (e.g. `+52…`, `+1…` patterns) that your sandbox tenant is configured to accept.
- For **duplicate contact** tests, reuse the same `phone` under the same `userToken` and expect a **400**-class error with a clear `reason` / `code` (see OpenAPI for `createContact`).

## CIP / KYC and session links

- Sandbox tenants may auto-promote certain CIP levels or require widget-based flows.
- Session link endpoints (`sessionlink-v2`, `sessionlink-v4`, etc.) often return a **`link`** URL; open it in a browser or headless flow as documented for your product.

## Webhooks and async events

- Register a **reachable HTTPS** URL (or tunnel such as ngrok) for webhook callbacks in non-production.
- Verify **signature / shared secret** handling if your integration consumes outbound events.
- Use a unique **`correlationId`** on money operations so support can correlate logs across systems.

## Idempotency and retries

- Treat **POST** money and funding operations as **non-idempotent** unless you have an explicit idempotency contract.
- On **500** or network errors, use **exponential backoff**; on **400**, fix the payload before retrying.
